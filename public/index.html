Twenty-Eight

Four players, partners sitting opposite. You are South; North is your partner.

The deck — 32 cards

Only eight ranks are used, and the order is the game's own: J > 9 > A > 10 > K > Q > 8 > 7. The Jack is the strongest card and the Nine beats the Ace.

The points — 28 of them

J = 3, 9 = 2, A = 1, 10 = 1; everything else is worth nothing. Across four suits that is exactly 28 — the whole game is a fight over those 28 points. The value is printed in gold on every card that has one.

Bidding

You see four cards and bid how many of the 28 points your side will take, from 16 upward. The winner picks a trump suit and places that card face down. Then the rest of the cards are dealt.

The concealed trump

Nobody knows the trump suit — not even the bidder's partner. Follow the suit led if you can. When you cannot, you may call for trump to have it turned up; from that card onward trump beats everything. A trump played before the reveal is just a discard.

If you call for trump, you must then play one if you hold it. The bidder's face-down card returns to their hand when trump is revealed.
